# M3.14b — fine-mesh duty confirmation and peak auxiliary screen

Parent M3.14: coarse 500 W full service, coarse 600–1000 W not full; joint 12/12 grid 1000 W full, 6/6 grid only 579.094 s. Independent bed/HX triplets include failed periodic gates, so their apparent convergence orders are not qualified. A saturated mixed outlet and zero deficit cannot establish convergence of the underlying thermal margin.

Predeclared campaign: 500, 1000 and exploratory 1250 W, joint 12/12 and 16/16 cells, 10 continuous cycles per case with identical 1200 s charge, 600 s discharge and K=2. Add half max step at 1000 W 12/12. Seven jobs independently execute every case and aggregate only complete evidence. 16 is the current solver's permitted grid limit. This is two-grid confirmation, not a new GCI triplet. Unmixed minimum HX outlet minus target and cooler/blower/heater accepted-node peaks are reported; they are not guaranteed continuous extrema. Do not replace the actual model service gate with a positive temperature margin.

A conservative analyst-selected screening gate requires both grids to pass periodic and full service; nonnegative raw accepted-node temperature margin; <=1 K absolute difference in raw margin; <=2% relative total modeled electrical-energy change and <=5% cooler peak change, referenced to 16 cells. These are screening limits, not established numerical uncertainty bounds. A nonpassing gate is a valid result and results must not be discarded. No monotonic capacity assumption or interpolation of a maximum; a higher duty may be untested. Independent 12-cell half-step at 1 kW must also pass full service, nonnegative raw margin, <=2% electrical-energy difference and <=1 K margin difference before 1 kW is eligible for sizing. Other duties have no fine-grid temporal corroboration in this campaign.

Only the strongest discrete passing pair can enter provisional sizing, using the 16-cell result. Retain actual cycle energy and coolant duty; add coincident peak cooler duty, coolant mass flow and assumed pump W, blower peaks, heater nameplate, rock inventory and HX conductance. Parallel modules remain hypothetical, synchronous, noncontinuous. 100 kPa pressure differential and 65% pump efficiency are assumptions. Common headers, actual sink feasibility, electrical distribution, valve losses outside the prototype and qualified heat transfer/hardware remain absent. No 1 MW system validation or economic claim. This is a comparison baseline for larger-vessel design, not permission to purchase equipment.
