# M3.19 — Source-bounded 150°C heat pump with electric boost versus retargeted TES charging

## Motivation and verified precedents

M3.16b (merged PR59) established model-only periodic 1 MW/150°C water process duty for 600 seconds, with a 190°C gas charging target, 3 MW heater per bank, 4 m assumed header, 500 W/K assumed total heat leakage per bank, 3 staggered banks, and 1.303752 MW average modeled electric input. The 190°C gas charging target is not equivalent to OST's experimentally demonstrated 150°C water sink. M3.18 (merged PR61) completed source-specific EOS screens using independent assumed compressor efficiency: for 80→60°C source, 110→150°C water sink, 2 MPa, R600a COP=2.302055; R600 COP=2.424726. No compressor map, hardware qualification or speed dependence. R290/R601a mixture heat-pump predictions currently unresolved due to CoolProp HEOS critical-state limitations; they must not be replaced with ideal mixtures.

## Compared architectures

**A. Direct high-temperature heat pump.** Gas charged toward 190°C demands at least 195°C charge-loop hot-water outlet for a 5 K exchanger approach. At an additional assumed 5 K refrigerant-to-water gas-cooler hot-end pinch, compressor discharge must reach 200°C or above. OST prototype's documented nominal 160°C discharge limit does not support this. Future higher-temperature compressors or multi-stage HP may be considered *only if* independent compressor maps, discharge-temperature ratings, pressure/safety compliance and matched-source temperature-glide heat-exchanger pinch are established. No COP or electric saving is claimed at 195°C.

**B. 150°C transcritical HP + resistive booster.** The charging model places the electric heater in the gas return, with the target 190°C. A 150°C hot-water preheater cannot warm gas beyond 145°C at the assumed 5 K approach. Each sampled actual-model charging state supplies return-gas temperature, gas mass flow and electric-heater heat rate. Compute the optimistic upper bound on recoverable 150°C preheat as min(existing gas heat, m_air*(h_air(145°C)-h_air(Treturn))) for Treturn below 145°C; otherwise zero. Integrate 10 s samples trapezoidally, require agreement within 4% of the actual independently integrated stage gas-heating ledger, cap energy at ledger. Substitute HP heat plus electric-booster heat for the *same* modeled gas heat and report corresponding conditional electricity using COP=2.302055 from the M3.18 **source80 R600a assumption-specific EOS screen** and assumed resistance-heater efficiency 95%. Do not assume compressor-map validation, actual HP heat transfer UA, hot-loop dynamics, refrigeration-cycle charge-time control, gas return pressure losses or availability of source heat. Thus the result is a **theoretical maximum HP contribution and electricity-saving upper bound**, not an implemented coupled transient or a guaranteed savings estimate. Source heat and electric HP energy are each accounted, with no additional heat created. No real air-like gas flow mixing/multi-stage hardware is resolved.

**C. Reduced-temperature TES charge.** Run the actual existing dynamic industrial-bed solver at gas heater setpoints 145,165,175,180,190°C with fixed 1 MW/150°C/600 s process discharge, 500 W/K bank heat loss, 3 MW heater nameplate/bank, 20 equivalent vessels/bank, fixed 4 m return headers, no cooler. 10 continuous cycles per case. The 145°C gas charge is compatible with a 150°C water source from a hot-end heat-transfer standpoint, but on the same thermal-storage schedule is unlikely to deliver 150°C water: use the model's *actual service and approximate periodicity gate* and do not force this outcome. Reduced heater setpoints 165/175/180°C require 170/180/185°C hot-water heat input if direct HP is considered; the OST 160°C discharge-rating hardware cannot supply those conditions with positive approach. Independent 12/16 cell and half-time-step sensitivity at 190 and 175°C, both judged against the same service. Lower targets have not been independently refined unless explicitly tested.

## Thermodynamic boundaries

The process (discharge) water is supplied at an assumed 2 MPa from 110°C to 150°C; a 150°C gas-charging water stream is not a substitute for 190°C gas at the heater. Independent M3.18 80°C-source pure-fluid screens cannot be equated to measured hardware performance; propane/isopentane mixture data requested from Tim Pfeiffer remain pending.

No automatic guarantee of four-quadrant Carnot-battery round-trip efficiency, electric-to-electric discharge (not modeled), auxiliary electricity for HP, compressor hardware, explosion protection or integrated HP–TES controls. A heat-only output-to-input ratio is not an electric Carnot-battery efficiency. Explicitly separate the physically simulated *electrical-resistance TES service* from a hypothetical external source-split upper bound.
