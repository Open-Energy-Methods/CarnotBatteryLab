# M3.16b — zero-cooling confirmation and main-system insulation sensitivity

## Evidence and decision
Exact M3.16 CI (run 44438773236) passed eight workflows, 130 cycles. Eleven cases qualified under model-only gates; both buffers failed periodicity, with only 0.611 MJ and 0.291 MJ returned versus 25.029 MJ and 26.420 MJ captured at 500 kW and zero cooler cap. No positive buffer benefit. Zero cooler at 5 MW per-bank heater: 1058467.019485 W mean electricity, 29.108 K raw margin, 462.577 K peak thermal temperature; 3 MW heater: 1058471.658548 W, 29.099 K, 462.574 K. Mean electricity differs by 4.64 W, but sampled peak plant input falls from 5.482 to 3.483 MW and nameplate from 15 to 9 MW. Do not optimize on numerical noise.

## Predeclared confirmation
Same 1 MW/150 C/600 s duty, 1200 s charge, 4 m header surrogate, ten continuous cycles per case, zero cooling, no buffer. Repeat both 5 and 3 MW per-bank heater cases at 16 and 12 cells jointly, independently halve maximum step at 16 cells. For 3 MW also quarter the time step and run 8 cells diagnostically. This is sensitivity screening, not asymptotic convergence or resolved adaptive timestep order. Same eligibility gates as M3.16; require both cases pass, <=2% mean electricity relative difference, <=1 K raw-margin and peak thermal difference, <=5% sampled peak electric relative difference for 12/16 and temporal checks. For confirmed cases use lower heater capacity when mean electric differs by at most 0.2% (analyst-chosen engineering tie band, not uncertainty quantification); otherwise choose lower mean consumption. 2 MW heater exploratory only.

## Main-system heat losses
The prior model is adiabatic except for explicit cooler and buffer losses. Add signed thermal fluxes to ambient at 25 C from rock cells, HX wall cells, and lumped return gas. Each uses Q=UA*(T-Tambient), distribute rock and wall UA uniformly over their cells. Include these terms directly in each component balance and integrate separate energy ledgers. Heat can flow inward if a component becomes colder than ambient; no rectification or unexplained energy sink. The existing state recurrence and each heat-loss ledger must repeat over the last two cycle boundaries. For 3 MW heaters, compare aggregate bank UA 100, 500, 1000 W/K with the no-loss case, allocated 60% rock, 20% HX wall, 20% return gas. These are intentionally assumed stress-test values, not source-verified insulation estimates. No new pressure drop or heat leakage geometry, no measured thermal bridges, no radiation model. Only matched eligible cases support meaningful energy comparison. No loss-case grid refinement yet. Actual plant insulation and standby losses need physical design and measurements; the adiabatic result is not an efficiency claim.

## Scope
The target water flow-exergy rise is retained from M3.16; complete component-level energy and entropy/exergy balances need separate work. Resistance heaters, not an 80 C source heat pump. No continuous industrial service, hardware qualification or Carnot battery round-trip efficiency. Numerical failures must be reviewed without relaxing gates to force a positive result.
