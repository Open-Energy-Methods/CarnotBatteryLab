# M1.8 — UDE measured n-butane instrumentation audit, independent-run holdout gate

**Evidence class:** original laboratory compressor sensor data audit and test-design eligibility, **not** a validated compressor efficiency map. The original H-REF target remains source 80→70°C, 110→150°C liquid process at 1 MW. This work tests **whether another laboratory's genuine recordings have enough independent operating conditions to justify an interpolation validation claim**.

## Primary data and physical rig

Schmeing, Welp & Atakan (University of Duisburg-Essen), ["Experimental Dataset: Heat Pump coupled to a Thermal Storage for charging a Carnot Battery"](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18712882), 2026. Original `data.zip`, **published MD5 `d17bf8b134977f4672c1ec22f085f0ea`**, 36.1 MB. The conference paper (doi:10.36227/techrxiv.176478421.10869135/v1) describes a **3.3 kW electrical reciprocating compressor** integrated with two unpressurized hot-water tanks; temperature-dependent sink is roughly 45–90°C and the heat source tap water near ambient. This is **not** a 1 MW R600 transcritical 80°C industrial waste-heat compressor or a gas/rock packed-bed TES.

The checksum-verified original archive contains 184 entries, including **Bitzer primary compressor logger files** for the n-butane runs **026, 027, 028**, dated 9–10 October 2025. Their filenames state 70 Hz and varying nominal storage temperature. Metadata discovery established **2,280, 2,310, 2,249 records, respectively**, totaling **6,839 time-correlated records**. The logger CSVs have **157, 155, 155 columns**, respectively, using semicolon separators. Columns explicitly label low/high **absolute** pressure as bar(a), compressor speed, compressor frequency and motor power (the latter two mechanical/electrical quantities must not be assigned SI units by guesswork).

## Corrected source inventory — six actual butane experiment summaries

**Independently reproduced directly from the checksum-verified original 66-column multirow experimental means table:** there are **17** original experiment entries: **six labeled `Butane`**, **four `Propane`**, and **seven `Propane * Pentane` / `Propane  * Pentane`**. All six n-butane rows contain most required recorded thermodynamic channels. Three have additional outlet-temperature sensor metadata. These **six experimental summary rows** are the appropriate independent *experiment-level units* for physically based off-target benchmarking, **not** the 6,839 timestamped device-controller records.

The same original ZIP provides only **three separately labeled n-butane Bitzer diagnostic logs** (026–028). They are **sparse asynchronous change/event logs**, not complete synchronized pressure/flow/power series. The compressor's controller `Refrigerant` field is populated only twice in each log and reports **R290**, even though the original run filenames and independently published experiment table identify butane. Its compressor low/high pressure channels each have only two nonblank records and **both are zero**; no Bitzer raw row passes simultaneous positive pressure, speed, frequency and motor-channel checks. This does **not** prove that propane was physically present in the butane experiment—the controller can retain a stale configured-fluid label—but it prohibits using those controller records as verified n-butane P–T–shaft maps. We retain the contradiction, do not impute missing channels and use the **author-described processed measured experiment summary** as a distinct, properly labeled evidence source.

The original processed column contract distinguishes **measured** and **calculated** variables. Direct, explicitly unit-labeled source columns are `wf_name` (working-fluid identity), `m_dot_wf` (kg/s), `p-R02` and `p-R03` (compressor suction/discharge Pa absolute), `T-R02` (K), `f_comp` (Hz electrical), and `P_comp_el` (W electric power at inverter inlet). The reported `n_comp` is **derived** from electrical frequency and two pole pairs; do not treat it as a separate tachometer measurement. Columns `eta_comp_global`, `eta_comp_is_T3_new`, and `eta_comp_vol` are **derived calculations**, not independent instrument measurements. The open source's separately archived compressor model has non-publication language and is not used, translated, or redistributed.

**Independent EOS test:** calculate the ideal compression enthalpy with CoolProp `HEOS::n-Butane` at independently reported suction P/T and discharge P. Form `eta_is,electric = m_dot_wf * (h2s - h1) / P_comp_el` using the source's directly unit-labeled measured channels and inverter electric meter boundary. Compare this *separately recomputed* efficiency with M1.7's original no-fit `0.68894` hypothesis and evaluate, for **each of the six experiment-summary records**, fixed reference, train-only constant, and pre-specified training-only log-pressure-ratio ridge. Never tune hyperparameters using withheld results or fit across other refrigerants. Divide the evaluation by **genuine same-electrical-frequency P/T/log-ratio convex-hull support**; no implicit interpolation between distinct motor frequency classes.

This is an **independent code-level cross-check of measured signals**, not an independent efficiency sensor, not independent statistical campaigns, and not a verified compressor operating map. The source compressor, working pressure, temperature range, capacity and auxiliary component topology are different from H-REF.

## Checksum-reproduced measured-source results — 16 November 2026

Independent CI downloaded **both original Zenodo sources**, checked their published MD5s, did not load the restricted compressor model, and reprocessed the author-described six pure-butane experiment means from their explicitly unit-labelled sensor columns.

- **6/6 pure-butane experiment means were physically admissible for independent CoolProp HEOS::n-Butane ideal-work evaluation**, with no missing required measured P/T/massflow/electric-power fields. They are original **experiment-summary averages**, not thousands of independent point samples.
- Recomputed overall electric isentropic efficiency `m_dot_wf*(h2s-h1)/P_comp_el`: **range 0.40491–0.49099, mean 0.44454**. This is a *derived efficiency computed by new code from the original measured sensor signals*; it does **not** constitute a separate experimental efficiency meter.
- **Original fixed M1.7 electrical-efficiency hypothesis 0.68894** predicts much higher efficiency at these **different, low-pressure UDE laboratory conditions**: six-case **MAE 0.24440**, **RMSE 0.24583** (absolute efficiency units). This strongly rejects using the same constant to describe *both* these distinct OST and UDE off-design compressor conditions, but **does not directly refute an undiscovered H-REF compressor's target-efficiency capability**.
- **Leave-one-original-experiment-out (6 withheld predictions)** with the original fixed constant gives MAE **0.24440**; the separately fitted, training-only mean gives MAE **0.02426**, and the preregistered two-parameter pressure-ratio ridge gives MAE **0.02691**. The simple training-mean fit is not demonstrably inferior to the ridge. Because **0/6** withheld source experimental conditions are in the *same-electric-frequency* three-dimensional training P/T/log(PR) convex hull, all six model tests are **out-of-support**. Thus we explicitly **do not claim validated in-envelope predictive accuracy**, notwithstanding the lower off-support numerical MAEs.
- Source compressor **suction pressure** range **106,958–133,787 Pa (1.070–1.338 bar absolute)** and **discharge** **571,919–1,094,743 Pa (5.719–10.947 bar absolute)**. These are far below the original M1.4 transcritical R600 requirements of roughly **7.199 bar suction** and **40–60 bar high side**. No claim of useful exact-service extrapolation is legitimate.
- The original, completely separate **OST five withheld condition hashes** are exactly `4f0a2b700ee73221`, `5dc9eee2bf90a3e0`, `7aac3574d2cbc688`, `7c72ebc272835679`, `86060d047e5b07fa`. Their original **MAE 0.06652**, 15 training observations, five heldout conditions, and the original single in-training-hull holdout are unchanged. **No cross-machine pooling or retroactive holdout selection** occurred.

The reported six-point train-only fits remain *exploratory retrospective cross-validation within one original research apparatus*, not a second independent future dataset. Full external predictive validation would require calibrated raw measured P/T/flow/electrical input, traceable uncertainties, sufficiently many independent stable runs spanning fixed speed lines, more than one truly interior heldout condition, and separate machine capacity/temperature rating evidence. No manufacturer map and no 1 MW H-REF architecture is qualified by these results.

## Predeclared strict data protocol

1. Verify the original unmodified ZIP bytes against its **publisher-recorded MD5** *before* reading any source. Compute source SHA-256 in the result report. No external source code is executed. The source ZIP contains an experimental compressor model with a README explicitly saying its underlying Roskosch model must **not be made public**. Do **not** import, copy, redistribute, translate, trace or execute that model without explicit permission.
2. Read exclusively three instrument-logger filenames containing both the **expected experiment ID** and literal `n-Butane`. Reject duplicates, missing run files, unsafe ZIP paths, wrong CSV widths or absent mandatory sensor names. A CSV row with malformed or absent values cannot be relabeled as a valid test.
3. Parse original logger numeric strings with decimal-comma support, check finite values and **positive** original absolute suction pressure, strictly greater discharge pressure, positive speed and frequency, and positive motor-power channel. Rows failing those checks are recorded as unavailable/filtered, **not imputed, calibrated or fitted**.
4. Aggregate **only per-run** valid sample counts, sensor ranges and medians. Each run is the largest defensible distinct experimental unit; thousands of within-run samples are serially correlated and must **not** become thousands of fake independent train/holdout groups. Even the three runs are from one instrumentation system and campaign (run-level withholding is not independent laboratory validation).
5. Predeclare **leave-entire-run-out** assessments: for each of runs 026/027/028, reserve that entire run, leaving the other two runs for fit eligibility. Independently check whether the reserved run's operating centroid (absolute suction pressure bar(a), absolute discharge pressure bar(a), raw logger compressor-speed channel) is on the **nonnegative line segment** between the **two available training-run centroids**. A two-point training set spans a *line*, **not a three-dimensional operating envelope**. Merely being between separate coordinate min/max values is insufficient.
6. The predeclared **screening heuristic** is at least **five independently conducted usable runs** and a true in-domain withheld run, with independently confirmed sensor meter boundaries and calibration uncertainties, before any claim of in-envelope predictive accuracy. Five is a conservative research protocol heuristic, **not a manufacturer/regulatory standard**. Three runs necessarily fail the sample-size gate; no compressor efficiency model is trained or evaluated on them, even when individual recorder rows are plentiful.
7. Preserve the **original OST M1.7 five withheld measured conditions**, source MD5, source-data SHA-256, original holdout grouping seed and original **0.066520 MAE**. M1.8 calls the existing M1.7 parser and splitter **without changing its split, model or thresholds**. Original OST data are *not* combined with UDE observations to train a cross-machine surrogate. The original OST holdouts are *not* re-selected, retuned, "improved" or counted as independent UDE experimental points.
8. Publish **only source provenance, run-level aggregate sensor summaries, split eligibility, pre-registered missing-field decisions and original OST hashed holdout identities**, not original third-party time series, PDF/model source text or raw sensor data.

## Physics and measurement limits

- `Low pressure [bar(a)]` / `High pressure [bar(a)]` directly declare **absolute** pressure; pressure ratio may be calculated from those two reported channel values for valid records. Not evidence the pressure sensors are calibrated or correct at the original H-REF target.
- `Compressor speed`, `Compressor frequency`, and `Motor power` logger fields are genuine recorded channels, but their **units, signal provenance and motor/shaft meter boundaries are not independently established here**. Do **not** promote `Motor power` into measured shaft power [W], electrical input [kW] or measured overall isentropic efficiency by assuming a device manufacturer's scaling.
- The separate processed UDE table may contain mass-flow/temperature/efficiency variables, but it has a **multirow header and separate sensor metadata**. Joining it to the Bitzer raw logger by date/run/time or treating a derived model quantity as an independent sensor requires a documented, independently checked alignment, time base, original calibration and units. This milestone deliberately does not invent the join or derive absent source measurements.
- Time samples from the same charging run may share control ramps, drift, sensor offsets, common transients, and changing storage temperature. Temporal autocorrelation and leakage are why arbitrary random-row holdouts would be scientifically misleading.
- A model with `Motor power` as the dependent variable would be uninterpretable until its units and electrical/shaft boundary are independently established. Therefore **no physical power-prediction RMSE, COP, isentropic-efficiency accuracy or manufacturer's compressor map is claimed**.
- The run-level pressure/speed screening is at **UDE** suction/sink conditions, not the industrial R600 80°C source / 150°C process envelope; it is an *external research validity test*, not a plausible 1 MW industrial compressor architecture.

## Decision gate

**Current external evidence:** six distinct source-labeled pure-butane measured experiment summaries with explicit P/T/flow/inverter-power columns, and three separate **invalid-for-synchronous-compressor-validation** sparse Bitzer controller logs. The six means pass the research protocol's *count* screen, but robust **independent in-envelope predictive accuracy requires multiple fully in-hull whole-experiment holdouts at matching electrical frequency**, documented calibration and sensor uncertainty, and an independently held-out campaign. In the absence of these checks, the correct status is **MEASURED_OFFTARGET_COMPRESSOR_EFFICIENCY_RECOMPUTED / IN_ENVELOPE_PREDICTION_UNVERIFIED**, not a deceptively accurate interpolation of correlated 6,839 instrument samples.

**Global architecture gate unchanged:** M1.5 original H-REF manufacturer map not verified; process-liquid heat exchanger and TES measured packed-bed breakthrough/pressure-loss validation pending; no matched-service 1 MW model or demonstrated round-trip efficiency.
